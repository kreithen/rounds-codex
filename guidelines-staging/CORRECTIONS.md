**replaced — original overstated a nonexistent trial**

The original cited a 2025 NEJM pragmatic trial proving intraoperative hyperoxia increases pulmonary complications, and said major societies had rewritten oxygen guidelines to a 92-96% SpO₂ target. No such trial was found and no such target has been adopted. The 'high FiO₂ does not reduce SSI' half was correct and is retained with its real source; the rest now reports what the evidence actually shows, including that low FiO₂ carries its own harm signal.
