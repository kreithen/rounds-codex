## Findings stated backwards — a reader would learn the opposite of the published result

### Anesthesiology 2025 #3 — Individualized Perioperative Blood Pressure Management in Patients Undergoing Major Abdominal Surgery: The IMPROVE-multi Randomized Clinical Trial

**replaced — original stated the opposite result**

The original cited an 'HPI-SMART Trial' that does not exist, said it showed individualized relative MAP targets significantly reduce AKI and myocardial injury, and said international hemodynamic standards were rewritten to recommend targeting within 10-15% of baseline. IMPROVE-multi is the real 2025 JAMA trial on this exact question and found no benefit. The entry now states the actual finding.

### Anesthesiology 2025 #10 — Viscoelastometric-Guided Early Fibrinogen Concentrate Replacement during Postpartum Haemorrhage (OBS2)

**replaced — original stated the opposite result**

The original cited a 2025 Lancet multicentre trial proving VHA-guided fibrinogen reduces ICU admissions and blood product exposure, and said obstetric hemorrhage standards now strongly recommend it over plasma. No such trial exists. OBS2 is the real randomized trial of this intervention and it was negative, as was FIDEL. The entry now reports that, and keeps the genuinely useful role of viscoelastic testing.

### Anesthesiology 2026 #2 — Intervals for Oscillometric Arterial Pressure Monitoring during Noncardiac Surgery: The 2.5-Minute versus 5-Minute Randomized Clinical Trial

**corrected — original stated the opposite result**

The trial is real (NCT06314074) and it was NEGATIVE. The original said it proved shorter intervals reduce hypotensive depth and duration, and told readers to shorten NIBP cycling to 2.5 minutes during induction and high-risk phases — the opposite of what the trial found, and a practice change based on a null result. Rewritten to the actual finding, with the norepinephrine confound that explains it.

### Cardiology 2025 #5 — Early Intervention in Patients With Asymptomatic Severe Aortic Stenosis and Myocardial Fibrosis (EVOLVED)

**corrected — original stated the opposite result**

The trial is real and correctly journaled and dated, but it was NEGATIVE. The original said early TAVR 'significantly lowers 3-year mortality and heart failure re-admission' and that guidelines shifted it from class IIb to class I. Neither is true: HR 0.79 (95% CI 0.44-1.43), P = .44, 'no demonstrable effect', and no guideline class change occurred. Two further points added: the intervention was aortic valve replacement generally, not TAVR specifically, and the trial recruited only 224 of 356 planned patients.

### Cardiology 2025 #8 — Left Atrial Appendage Closure versus Best Medical Therapy in AF at High Risk of Stroke and Bleeding (CLOSURE-AF)

**corrected — original stated the opposite result**

The trial is real (AHA, 8 November 2025, Landmesser, 912 patients, 42 sites) but the result is the REVERSE of the original entry. The original said it proved noninferiority for stroke prevention with a dramatic bleeding reduction, and that guidelines now recommend LAAC as an early first-line alternative to NOACs at high HAS-BLED. LAAO did not meet noninferiority; medical therapy including anticoagulation was better. The comparator was also best medical care, not NOACs alone.

### Dermatology 2025 #7 — Emollients to Prevent Pediatric Eczema: A Pragmatic and Decentralized Randomized Clinical Trial (CASCADE)

**corrected — population and formulation claims reversed**

The trial is real and POSITIVE (JAMA Dermatology, 23 July 2025, 1,247 dyads, 25 clinics). Two substantive corrections. The original said the trial clarified 'which formulations (ceramide-dominant vs standard petrolatum)' benefit — it made no formulation comparison; a single bland moisturizer was used. And the original attributed the benefit to 'infants with genetic skin-barrier susceptibility' — CASCADE explicitly did NOT select for AD risk, and trials in atopy-prone infants have been negative, so the population claim was backwards.

### Dermatology 2025 #8 — Nicotinamide for Skin Cancer Chemoprevention: A Real-World Cohort Study

**corrected — transplant claim reversed**

Real (JAMA Dermatology 2025, DOI 10.1001/jamadermatol.2025.3238, VA Corporate Data Warehouse, 33,822 patients). One important correction: the original said the study 'Solidifies real-world chemoprevention guidelines for immunocompetent AND high-risk organ transplant populations' — the cohort found NO overall benefit in transplant recipients. Added the timing gradient, which is the study's central and most actionable finding and was absent from the original, and the accompanying commentary noting the question remains unsettled.

### Cardiology 2025 #9 — Colchicine in Acute Myocardial Infarction (CLEAR SYNERGY / OASIS 9)

### Dermatology 2026 #7 — Stapokibart for the Treatment of Refractory Palmoplantar Pustulosis

**NOT FOUND**

But NO publication on palmoplantar pustulosis was located in the searches run — searches returned only JAK inhibitor and apremilast case reports for refractory PPP, and reviews citing IL-17, IL-23, IL-36 and IL-1β as the targeted approaches. The claim of 'significant clearing of painful palmar and plantar pustules' and 'trial evidence for IL-4Rα blockade' is unsupported by anything found, and has been reduced to a neutral description. Verify this citation exists before shipping it.
