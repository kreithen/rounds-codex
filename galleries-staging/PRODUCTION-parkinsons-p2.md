The SNr leader is the one that travels furthest to get there: it leaves its label as a horizontal at

(477,620), (461,608), (453,602) — to finish on the sphere. The Red nucleus leader makes the same
crossing one row lower, elbow at **(467,690)**.

**A reader who trusts this page learns that the thalamus, the substantia nigra and the red nucleus
are the same structure.** Of the three, only the last is right.

*Note on the fourth tip:* two nearly parallel shallow leaders run right from this sphere toward the
Thalamus label at y ≈ 549 — one from the dot at (396,551), one from a tip at (389,564). They
converge around x 440 and only one horizontal reaches the label. We could not separate them where
they cross the cerebellum's own bright folia. **Please check at source whether the Thalamus label
carries two leaders**; if it does, both need moving.

### Where SNr should go — and the problem with that

The substantia nigra **is not drawn as a distinct structure in the sagittal panel.** The midbrain and
pons are one undifferentiated tan column, x 330–395, y 540–640. The **SNc** label (left column,
amber) already lands inside that column at ≈ (345,568) with nothing to mark, and SNr has nowhere at
all to point.

So this row is not a leader move: **the sagittal panel needs a substantia nigra band drawn into the
midbrain**, with SNc and SNr as its two layers, before either label means anything. The page's own
**SUBSTANTIA NIGRA (MIDBRAIN)** panel already draws exactly that — it can be the reference.

## 5. The three [CHECK] rows, resolved

**Row 7, `Cingulate gyrus` — CONFIRMED as a defect.** Its tip is at **(290,420)**, *inside* the purple
callosal band. **Corpus callosum** ends at **(256,452)** in the same band, 43 px away. Two labels,

about 15 px up and out from where it is.

**Row 9, `Subthalamic nucleus (STN)` — CONFIRMED, and worse than the order states.** The green leader
passes straight over the olive-green sphere (centre **(317,566)**, x 306–328 y 555–578) and stops at

sphere; we measure **33 px past its centre**, clear of its edge entirely. The sphere is the only
green object in the region and matches the label's own green ink, so the intended target is not in
doubt.

**Row 10, `Internal capsule` — CONFIRMED, and not fixable by moving the leader.** Hooked tip at
**(377,512)**, sitting exactly on the seam between the purple ovoid and the blue body. **No
white-matter lamina is drawn anywhere in this panel.** Like SNr above, this label needs artwork
before it needs a leader: either draw a capsular band between the pallidum and the thalamus, or drop
the label from the sagittal view and carry it on the coronal panel, which has the room.

**Row 8, `Motor cortex (Precentral gyrus)` — OPEN, please check at source.** The tip is at
**(253,356)**, on the frontal convexity roughly a third of the way back from the frontal pole, with
the vertex at x ≈ 330–360. That reads as too far forward — but **no central sulcus is drawn on this
brain**, so there is no landmark on the page to measure against and we will not call it. Your
renderer knows where the precentral gyrus was meant to be; we do not.
