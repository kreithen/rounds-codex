## What the project is

Gallery anatomy pages carry labels joined to the artwork by leader lines. **On a large fraction of
pages the line does not end on the structure the label names.** That teaches the wrong anatomy, and
it is the defect Dr. Kreithen reported.

Measured across the pages checked so far:

| | |
|---|---|
| pages examined | **119** |
| **wrong** — a line lands on the wrong structure | **81** |
| suspect — could not be called either way | 17 |
| clean | 21 |
| individual label findings | **553** |

The earlier subset that went into the production brief put it at **323 of 1,098 labels — 30%.**

## Three rules that are not negotiable

1. **NEVER repaint delivered artwork.** A dot-row repainter was written once. It detected the row by
   autocorrelation, sampled colours off the page, rebuilt the background by interpolation, and
   refused pages it could not read. It still produced half-erased "U"-shaped dots, skipped 31 of 80
   pages as unreadable, and was deleted. **Flag for re-render instead.**
   *The narrow exception, already used:* `scripts/transplant_gallery_text.py` and
   `scripts/fix_gallery_icd.py` copy glyphs that **already exist elsewhere in the same batch** at the
   same size and font onto a background measured flat. Nothing is detected, invented or
   reconstructed. Read their docstrings before using them, and do not widen what they do.
2. **The physician is the medical gate.** `LABEL-QA.md` is a candidate list. The agents were told to
   report SUSPECT rather than guess, so the SUSPECT section is *expected* to contain correct pages.
   Nothing goes to production as a confirmed defect without his sign-off.
3. **Look at the image.** Every finding in this project came from reading a page by eye. The
   measurement failures below all came from trusting a number instead.

## Higgsfield: the alternative to a production re-render

`HIGGSFIELD-CORRECTION-PILOT.md` answers the physician's question — **yes, a mislabelled panel can be
AI-corrected**, at 18 credits over three runs on `pericarditis` p2. Two things make it work:

- **Crop out just the panel.** Run 1 sent the whole page and it regenerated the entire page.
- **One change per run.**

The physician's stated preference is to **regenerate only the incorrect panel and photoshop it into
the page himself.** An `aortic-dissection` anatomy panel was regenerated on that basis and he said he
would photoshop it when back at his desktop — **check whether that came back before starting new
generations.**

**Higgsfield spends the physician's credits. Do not run a batch without explicit approval past a
validated pilot.** The connector is also frequently unavailable; see the connector notes in
`CLAUDE.md`.

## Measurement traps, all paid for already

- **A dot count read off a downscaled image is wrong.** Three findings were retracted this way in one
  day — `hip-fracture` pages 1, 3 and 6, plus `back-pain` page 5. Two of those retractions took a
  headline conclusion with them.
- **Full-resolution pages are already on disk.** The physician pastes them from his photo album into
  chat and they land in `/root/.claude/uploads/<session-id>/` at **1024×1536**. An entire session was
  lost asking for files that were already there. Use `scripts/ingest_pasted_gallery.py`.
- **Control before you conclude.** A PDF logo comparison gave three confident wrong answers (91, then
  88, then 4 galleries affected) before it gave the right one. Control for size, then position, then
  look by eye.
- **A guard that passes on the broken build is decoration.** Run any new checker against the pre-fix
  file and confirm it fails.

## Branch discipline — this matters

The main build conversation works on **`claude/usmle-rounds-codex-module-bmpl61`**. Two conversations
pushing there will collide, and `legal/README.md` already records that lesson.

**Use `claude/anatomy-label-corrections`** for this project. Branch it from the same base:

```sh
git fetch origin && git checkout -b claude/anatomy-label-corrections origin/claude/usmle-rounds-codex-module-bmpl61
```

**Do not deploy to `rounds-codex-app` from this conversation without saying so first.** The web launch
is targeted at **17 August** and the other conversation is shipping to `main`; an unexpected deploy
from here lands in the middle of that. See `LAUNCH-AUG17.md`.
