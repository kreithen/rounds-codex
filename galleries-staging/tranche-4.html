Leader-line corrections — corrected work order, tranche 4

6 pages measured against the shipped artwork · 11 August 2026

Tranche 4 covers the six biggest remaining pages: pneumothorax, cdiff, hepatitis, ckd, gerd and hypothyroid, all page 2. Sixty-three rows were flagged across them; fifty-five are measured and confirmed. Thirty of the sixty-three were the auditors' [CHECK] calls, and twenty-eight of those turned out to be real.

All eight unverifiable rows are on the two pages that ship at 800×1200 — four on pneumothorax, four on hypothyroid. Every row on the four standard-size pages was measurable. Section 1 gives the pixel measurement behind that, because it is now a blocker rather than a preference.

What is not settled

ckd p2's renal blood supply series. Measured tips in label order: Renal artery (828,763), Segmental (848,760), Interlobar (863,768), Arcuate (838,786), Interlobular (831,822). The kidney is sectioned at x 780–840, so renal → segmental → interlobar runs outward when anatomically it runs inward — the first three are reversed along the vessel. Underneath that, all five tips are on the extrarenal trunk and its branches; interlobar belongs between the pyramids, arcuate at the corticomedullary junction, interlobular in the cortex, and none of them enters the kidney. Gold and orange vessels are drawn inside the outline at roughly x 780–800, y 765–800. Please confirm whether those are drawn as distinct interlobar, arcuate and interlobular vessels. If not, this needs artwork, and the honest alternative is to keep only Renal and Segmental on this figure.

gerd p2's LES callout box. The overview panel's LES leader ends at (133,470), the corner of a rectangular region box spanning x 68–133, y 420–472 — which encloses the whole stomach and distal oesophagus. Read as a region callout that is a legitimate style; read as a pointer it says the LES is the size of the stomach. We recommend a leader onto the sphincter and keeping the box if it is wanted, but this is a style decision and it is yours.

gerd p2's GE Junction row. We could resolve the label's mark at (108,1032) but not trace a leader further into the figure. Passed through as the order states it; if confirmed, the target is the squamocolumnar junction at about (72,1018).
