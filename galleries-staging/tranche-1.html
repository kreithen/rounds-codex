Glomerulus and Bowman's Capsule are each displaced onto the other's structure, and the capsule ring itself carries no label. They cannot simply be swapped end for end: with the correct endpoints, the Glomerulus leader has to pass over the capsule to reach the tuft inside it.

Blood supply panel — the order's "series is effectively inverted" is confirmed. Segmental ends (806,849), Interlobar (794,870), Arcuate (789,918). Those should run medial to lateral in that order and run exactly backwards. Interlobular (838,961) is correct. Segmental is the worst: the thick first-order trunks are at x 750–790 and its leader stops 20–50 px lateral of them. The Afferent Arteriole leader crosses the renal vein.

The seminal vesicle on this figure is labelled "Detrusor muscle" and the bladder wall is labelled "Seminal vesicle". They approach from opposite sides, so this is not a swapped pair — each is displaced in a way that lands on the other's target.

Worth knowing before re-rendering: the prostate spans x 690–780, split at the midline urethra at x ≈ 728, and the pubic symphysis is a separate pale ovoid at x 645–680. At low magnification they read as one mass, which is very likely how "Pubic symphysis" ended up on the penile root.

Testis and Scrotum are layout problems. The penis is drawn between the label column and the testis, so correct leaders would visibly run through it — please re-site those two labels rather than lengthening their leaders. Six further rows on this page are carried over unverified.
