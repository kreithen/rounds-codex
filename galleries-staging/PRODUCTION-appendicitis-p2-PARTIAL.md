out**, which in our measured stack would be the muscularis. We traced that tip to **(160,821)**, which
is **r = 81 of a 77 radius** — i.e. at or just outside the **outer rim**, which is where serosa
belongs.

not settle which is right. The panel is slightly oval and our ray was horizontal through the centre,
so the vertical radius may genuinely be larger than 77 — in which case the order's 110 could be
measured on a vertical and both readings could be describing the same point.

**Do not act on either reading until this is settled.** The other tips we traced, for the record:
