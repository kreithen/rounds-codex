Leader-line corrections — corrected work order, tranche 2

8 pages measured against the shipped artwork · 11 August 2026

Tranche 2 covers the eight pages our triage ranks worst — ranked by what the error would teach, not by how many labels each page has flagged. Every coordinate was taken from the shipped page at 6× or better; ordering and side claims at 11× or better, on a crop containing the whole series.

Two new faults have been added to the brief since tranche 1 — fault 4 (on a layered figure, every layer label stops in the middle layer) and fault 5 (the leader lands on a colour the page's own key assigns to something else). Fault 5 is the only one of the five you can check automatically. Please re-read the brief before starting this batch.

1

Wrong side, wrong vessel, wrong organ

sepsis p2 — the BRAIN marker is on the chest wall

Three faults that are almost certainly one event. Callout ⑥ BRAIN ends at a green marker at (620,472), the left upper chest, about 225 px below a brain drawn at x 450–560, y 215–290. The correct marker sits beside the temple at (565,247) and is orphaned — its stub dies at (642,268), exactly where it crosses the red BLOODSTREAM leader. Callout ⑤ BLOODSTREAM has no terminator at all; its leader fades into the chest. The green line looks to have been cut at the crossing and the remainder re-aimed.

bronchiolitis p2 — "Left main bronchus" is in the patient's right lung

The carina is at (472,625). "Left main bronchus" ends at ~(408,660), in the viewer's-left — the patient's right — lung, the same lung the "Right main bronchus" leader goes into. Underneath that: neither leader reaches a bronchus. Both stop in parenchyma 50–90 px short of the ringed tubes at x 415–520. The left main runs down-right from the carina to about (494,634), the right main to (444,634).

dvt p2 — the popliteal vein is marked on the tibia

Dot at (382,678); the knee joint line is at y = 605. That is 73 px below it, on the proximal tibial shaft — while the page's own caption for that label reads "Located in the popliteal fossa behind the knee." "Valve closed" cannot be corrected by moving it: no valve in that panel is drawn closed, all three cusp pairs are swept open. Draw one or drop the label.
