A short tranche, sent early on purpose. These four pages are the ones where a reader acts on the label and is wrong: cerebral arteries in the mediastinum, the vagina labelled inside the rectum, the heart and aorta in a lung, the placenta on the baby's head. Twenty-four rows; 20 measured and confirmed, 2 carried, 2 held for the physician.

These did not come from the original triage — they came from re-reading it. Our first ranking put all four in the bottom tier, because that tier was defined by exclusion and never actually read. Re-reading all 37 remaining pages found two tier-1-grade pages, about 22 with whole-structure errors, and only about 8 genuine near-misses. Do not treat the rest of the order as cosmetic.

5

Two things we are holding, and why

The umbilical vessel colours on gdm p2 — do not touch this panel yet. The PLACENTA (ENLARGED) panel labels an umbilical vein and umbilical arteries, and the order reports both leaders on the wrong vessels. But in the umbilical circulation the colour convention is inverted relative to the rest of the body: the vein carries oxygenated blood and is conventionally drawn red, the arteries carry deoxygenated blood and are drawn blue. So either the panel's colours are wrong (recolour the vessels, leave the leaders) or the leaders are wrong (move the leaders, leave the colours). Opposite fixes, and we cannot tell which from a render. Dr. Kreithen is looking at it.

The placenta may not be drawn as a distinct disc on either obstetric page. On both preeclampsia p2 and gdm p2 the PLACENTA label lands on the uterine wall, and on both we could not isolate a placental disc with an edge we would put a coordinate on. Two obstetric pages, same gap — worth one look at how the placenta is being drawn across the OB module before either page is re-rendered.

Two rows on labor p2 are carried unmeasured: Umbilical cord (order: dark-red placental tissue, ~30 px from the coiled cord) and Endometrium (order: the outer convex surface of the uterine globe, ~55 px from the cut edge where the lining is drawn). The second is worth your eye independently — on a three-layer panel, a label for the inner lining landing on the outer surface is fault 4, and the other two labels on that panel should be checked at the same time.
