Leader-line corrections — corrected work order, tranche 3

10 pages measured against the shipped artwork · 11 August 2026

Tranche 3 covers the second tier of the triage: parkinsons, migraine, schizophrenia, osteomyelitis, seizure, suicide, hyperkalemia, nephrolithiasis, uti and tb, all page 2. Fifty rows were flagged across them. Forty-seven are measured and confirmed as defects; the three we are not calling are set out in section 5, with the reason in each case. Twenty-three of the fifty were the auditors' [CHECK] calls — flagged rather than asserted — and twenty of the twenty-three turned out to be real.

A sixth fault, and it is the one that cannot be fixed by moving a line. Five labels across four pages in this batch name a structure that is not drawn on the page at all. Section 2 lists them. Please read that section before scheduling the batch, because those five need artwork, not a re-route, and they will otherwise come back as "cannot locate target".

3

Leaders that are not pointers

A different failure from a leader landing in the wrong place: here the line does not function as a pointer at all.

seizure p2 — an arrowhead pointing outward at its own label

In the THALAMO-CORTICAL NETWORK panel, Cortical networks starts at (531,891) on the right edge of the blue thalamus ovoid and its arrowhead is at the far end, (583,891), pointing right at the label text. The Cortex leader one line above shows the intended form: horizontal at y = 831, terminator at (441,831) on the pink cortical band. Reverse the arrow and land it on the band, about (555,845).

hyperkalemia p2 — one bracket doing two jobs, and a label with no line

A square bracket at x = 872 spanning y 406–533 is fed by two horizontals — "Loop of Henle" at y = 438 and "Ascending Limb" at y = 504 — while "Descending Limb" at y ≈ 462 has no leader at all. So neither limb is individually identified on the panel the whole page depends on. Keep the bracket for Loop of Henle; give each bullet its own short leader — descending limb (891,470), ascending limb (911,470). One caution: the ascending limb is drawn behind the vasa recta for most of its length, which is why it reads as absent. Bring it clear, or land the leader at y ≈ 420 or y ≈ 550 where it is visible.

5

What is not settled, and page sizes

Three of the fifty rows are not called as defects. One is unverifiable, two are judgement calls we are handing back rather than deciding for you.

The unverifiable one is unverifiable because of the export size. On tb p2, "Hilar lymph node enlargement" ends at (391,874), about 8 px lateral of the olive node cluster at x 380–392, y 870–912. The order reports ~20 px. At 800 px wide the nodes and the surrounding parenchyma no longer separate by colour, and at 24× the region is pixel mush — so we can neither confirm nor refute it. Please check it against your 1024×1536 master; if the tip is off the node, move it to (383,874).

tb p2 ships at 800×1200 against the 1024×1536 standard, and every tb coordinate in this document is in the shipped page's own pixels — multiply by 1.28 for standard-size equivalents. The other nine pages in this tranche are already at standard size. Please fix labels and size in one pass.

Two rows we are declining to call, and why. parkinsons "Motor cortex (Precentral gyrus)" ends at (253,356) on the frontal convexity, which reads as too far forward — but no central sulcus is drawn, so there is no landmark on the page to measure against and we will not guess. osteomyelitis "Epiphyseal arteries" at (297,1130) sits 35 px from "Metaphyseal arteries" on the same stretch of cortex; labelling an artery where it enters the bone is a legitimate convention, so the fix we ask for is separating the two so a reader can tell which foramen is which — not moving one to the epiphysis.

One near-miss on our side, recorded so you do not repeat it. On osteomyelitis p2 the bone's own silhouette carries a pale blue-grey highlight along x 391–405 in the distal third. It reads as a vein at low zoom and is not one — it traces the outline exactly. We nearly gave it as the target for "Venous drainage". The real venous channels run in the medullary cavity at x ≈ 341–348 and x ≈ 383–392.
