# How each footer wording is classified

The eight readers transcribed the wording accurately but applied the WRONG/OTHER labels
inconsistently — one counted "REVIEW / TREATMENT" as wrong where others called the same shape
OTHER. Their verdict columns are therefore NOT comparable and are not used. Classification is
done centrally, here, off the transcribed string, so one rule applies to all 780 pages.

## A — FALSE REVIEW CLAIM (must be corrected)

The cell asserts that clinical review has not happened. The physician has reviewed every page, so
these are factually wrong and are the defect that was reported.

    CLINICAL PENDING          Clinical Pending          CLINICAL PEERED
    CLINICAL + PATH PENDING   "Pathology pending"

Includes the hybrid pages where the RC shield IS present but captioned "CLINICAL PENDING" instead
of "Verified" — the mark is right and the caption is wrong.

## B — GARBLED WORD (wrong text, but not a review claim)

    CLINICAL PEARSONS

Appears on hepatitis and appendicitis. Almost certainly a corruption of "CLINICAL PEARLS". It
makes no false claim about review, but it is not a word, so it belongs on the re-render list.

## C — CONTENT DESCRIPTOR (no false claim; changing it is an editorial choice, not a fix)

The cell is labelled REVIEW but carries a section or tier name rather than a review status:

    HIGH-YIELD FOUNDATION   HIGH-YIELD CLINICAL   CORE CONCEPTS   ANATOMY FOUNDATION
    PATHOPHYSIOLOGY   CLINICAL PRESENTATION   PHYSICAL EXAM   DIAGNOSTICS   HISTOLOGY
    GROSS PATHOLOGY   TREATMENT   TREATMENT & FOLLOW-UP   CLINICAL PEARLS
    CLINICAL PEARLS & FOLLOW-UP   Start of Module   Early in Module   End of Module
    Anatomy & Physiology   Endoscopy & Management   HIGH YIELD USMLE / NCLEX

These do not say anything untrue. Whether they should ALSO become a verified badge is a design
decision for the physician, and is deliberately kept separate from the correction.

## D — ALREADY CORRECT

    RC shield + "Rounds Codex Verified"    (some pages also print a "Verification:" label)

## E — NO STATUS CELL

The template has none. hypoglycemia is the clear case: its rightmost footer element is an ICD-10
code. Nothing to correct.

## Unresolved: which wording is canonical?

The live correct pages say **"Rounds Codex Verified"** under an **RC** shield.
The physician's own screenshot of the correct artwork says **"MedCodex Verified"** under an **MC**
shield. Those are two different marks, and the audit cannot decide between them — it is a
branding decision. No page in the live set carries the MC/MedCodex form.
