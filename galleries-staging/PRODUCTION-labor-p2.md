## 1. The right-hand column is displaced one position — and it is a clean chain

The three labels down the right of the main sagittal figure each land on **the structure named by the
label above them.**

| label (top to bottom) | label y | terminator at | what is under it |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Sacrum** | 256 | **(732,341)** | **the posterior elements of a LUMBAR vertebra** |
| **Rectum** | 456 | **(722,472)** | **the sacrum** |
| **Vagina ③** | 516 | **(650,520)** | **inside the RECTUM** |

**Row 3, Sacrum.** At 6× the terminator sits on a vertebral lamina with **intervertebral discs
immediately above it (y ≈ 340) and below it (y ≈ 383)** — grey disc spaces, clearly drawn. That makes
it a mobile lumbar segment, not the fused sacrum. The sacrum begins below the last disc at y ≈ 400.
**Correct endpoint: (700,470)**, the sacral body.

**Row 2, Rectum.** The terminator is on the vertebral column at x 690–765 — the sacrum, i.e. exactly
where the Sacrum label belongs. **Correct endpoint:** the rectal lumen, around **(650,500)**.

**Row 1, Vagina.** The terminator is inside the large dark cavity at **x 615–700, y 455–545**. That
cavity is the rectum, and the page proves it itself: the canal descends continuously from it to
**(598,610)**, which is where the **Anus** label correctly lands.

**So the page tells a reader that the vagina is the rectum**, on the figure that teaches the birth
canal. **Correct endpoint:** the vaginal canal running down and forward from the cervix, anterior to
the rectum — approximately **(580,590)**. *Please place this one from the source file rather than our
coordinate:* the vagina, urethra and rectum run close together at this scale and we would rather you
picked the lumen than have us guess between them.

Fixing all three is one operation: **shift the right-hand column's leaders down one structure.**

## 2. `Bladder` is on the pubic bone

Row 4, confirmed. Terminator at **(475,559)**, on the **cream trabecular bone** at x 465–520,
y 545–600 — cortical rim and marrow both visible at 5×, with the **symphysis joint below it** at
y ≈ 600, where the **Pubic symphysis** label correctly lands at **(495,601)**.

So the bladder label and the pubic symphysis label are on the same bone, 42 px apart.

**Correct endpoint:** the bladder lumen, posterior and superior to the pubis. *We could not
confidently isolate a separately drawn bladder at this scale* — at term the uterus fills the pelvis
and the bladder is compressed against the pubis. **Please check at source: if the bladder is not
drawn as a distinct lumen, this becomes an artwork row, not a leader move.**

where the uterine cavity meets the vaginal canal.

Carried at the order's reading; the endpoint is measured and matches.
