## Panel 2, CAROTID & VERTEBROBASILAR SYSTEMS

The panel draws two red trunks ascending the neck, with the face to the right:

- **anterior trunk, x ≈ 160–168** — the common carotid. It **bifurcates at about (163,800)**: one limb
  continues up at x 158–166 to the skull base giving no facial branches (the **ICA**), the other fans
  anteriorly into the maxillofacial region at x 168–200, y 780–830 (the **ECA**).
- **posterior trunk, x ≈ 143–153** — ascends straight into the skull base without branching. That is
  the **vertebral artery**.

| row | label | ends at | what is there |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Internal Carotid Artery (ICA)** | **(146,768)** | the **vertebral artery** |
| 2 | **External Carotid Artery (ECA)** | round dot at **(145,808)** | the **vertebral artery**; the leader's final dash reaches x ≈ 158–160, the **common** carotid *below* the bifurcation |

So on the panel that exists to separate the carotid and vertebrobasilar systems, **both carotid
labels are on the vertebral artery** — and the vessel that actually bifurcates, 15–20 px to their
right, carries neither.

**Correct endpoints:** ICA → about **(161,770)**, the post-bifurcation limb with no facial branches.
ECA → about **(178,800)**, on the anterior branch fanning into the face. Both are unambiguous once
the bifurcation at (163,800) is used as the landmark.

Row 2 has a second defect worth fixing at the same time: its leader is **dashed and terminates
twice**, once in a round dot at (145,808) and again as a dash at x ≈ 158. A leader with two endpoints
gives the reader two answers.
