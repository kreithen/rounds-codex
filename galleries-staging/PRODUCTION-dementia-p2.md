Rows 1, 2 and 3 are a **three-way rotation**: corpus callosum → cingulate, cingulate → thalamus,
thalamus → corpus callosum. Each label names the structure the label above it is standing on.

**Correct endpoints:** Corpus callosum → **(250,442)**; Cingulate gyrus → **(250,415)**; Thalamus →
**(270,478)**; Mammillary body → the paired body below the hypothalamus, anterior to the midbrain;
Amygdala → the anterior medial temporal region; Brainstem → **(290,600)**, the gold column.

**Row 4 needs artwork, not a leader.** We could not find a separately drawn fornix anywhere on the
sagittal figure — the thin arch beneath the corpus callosum is not differentiated from the callosal
band. That makes it a **fault-6 row**: draw the fornix, or fold it into the corpus callosum label as
the label text already does ("fornix beneath").

## 2. `Hippocampus` has two terminators, and they straddle the boundary

Row 6, and it is worth a separate note because a plain confirm would be wrong.

At 18× the Hippocampus leader ends in **two ring terminators**:

- an inner ring at **(376,566)**, sitting on the **green hippocampus's outer rim**
- an outer ring at **(388,573)**, on the **pink arbor-vitae cerebellum**

So the label is **ambiguous** rather than plainly wrong — the work order read the outer ring, we can
see both. Either way the fix is the same and it removes the ambiguity: **one terminator, well inside
the green body — e.g. (330,545).**

This is the **third two-tip leader** we have measured (after `stroke` p2's ECA and `cdiff` p2's Right
colic a. and Inferior mesenteric v.). It is worth checking as a class: a leader with two endpoints
gives the reader two answers.

## 4. A colour contradiction across the page's own panels

Not in the work order, and worth fixing at the same time.

The **KEY STRUCTURES & FUNCTIONS** panel colours the **hippocampus green** and the **entorhinal
cortex gold**. The **sagittal** figure follows that — its hippocampus is the green C. The **coronal**
figure does not: there the hippocampus is **purple**, and gold is used for the entorhinal band only.

A reader who learns the key from the right-hand panel and then reads the coronal is being shown the
hippocampus in a colour the page has assigned to nothing. This is **fault 5** — the page contradicting
its own key — and it is the one class of defect that can be checked automatically. Row 7's note in
the work order gestures at the same thing from the other direction.

## 5. Row 14 — carried, not measured

**Nucleus basalis of Meynert** in the CHOLINERGIC PATHWAY thumbnail. The order reports the leader
ending on the **midbrain/upper brainstem** rather than on the bright orange basal-forebrain node
below the genu, where all the cholinergic fibres in that thumbnail converge. We did not re-measure it
and are passing it through as stated.
