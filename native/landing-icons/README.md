# An `apple-touch-icon` for `landing/` — prepared, deliberately NOT pushed

Written 2026-09-16. **This file belongs on `main`, in the `landing/` site that deploys
roundscodex.com. That tree is a disjoint history owned by the
`claude/rounds-codex-continued-qoh9b1` conversation, so nothing here has been pushed to it.**
Hand it over rather than having two sessions edit one area — the same arrangement as
`native/landing-aasa/`.

## The change

Replace **`landing/assets/apple-touch-icon.png`** with the file beside this README. Nothing else:
the `<link rel="apple-touch-icon" sizes="180x180" href="assets/apple-touch-icon.png">` tag in
`landing/index.html` already points at that path, and `landing/favicon.svg` is a separate asset that
this does not touch.

## Why — one aesthetic reason and one that is an actual defect

**The marks do not match.** roundscodex.com wears a *thick solid gradient ring* on a rounded square;
the app wears the *glowing thin ring* with the lens flare. Two different marks for one product, and
someone who saves both the landing page and the app to a home screen gets them side by side.

**And the shipped file has white corners.** It is RGB — no alpha — with **1,594 pure-white pixels,
4.9% of the image**, baked into the four corners outside its own rounding. iOS masks an
apple-touch-icon with its own squircle, which does not follow that rounding, so those corners show
as **white wedges** on the home screen against a dark icon.

That is the same class of fault the app's icon carried until v143, in the opposite direction: that
one was *transparent*, and iOS composites a transparent apple-touch-icon onto black before applying
the squircle, giving black wedges. Apple wants a **full-bleed opaque square** and does the rounding
itself. The file here is exactly that.

## Provenance

Generated by **`scripts/build_app_icons.py`**, from `scripts/logo-trim.png`, as the seventh output
of that script. It is **byte-identical to the app's own `icons/apple-touch-icon.png`** (md5
`c82e11c2ae…`), which is the point: one generator, one source, one mark.

The generator refuses to emit an icon whose mark is not square to within 10% or centred to within
2%. That check exists because every shipped app icon carried a **cropped** ring — the right arc
missing, so the emblem read as a "C" — from launch until 2026-09-16, and all four measured **0.78**
identically, which is what proved one upstream crop propagating rather than four mistakes. Full
account in `BRAND-ASSET-AUDIT.md`.

**Do not hand-edit this file.** If the lockup ever changes, change `logo-trim.png` and re-run
`python3 scripts/build_app_icons.py <web-clone> --apply`.

## What is deliberately left for that conversation to decide

Whether roundscodex.com *should* wear the app's mark at all. It is the marketing site, and a
marketing site choosing a flatter, higher-contrast icon is a defensible design call rather than an
oversight — the white corners are the part that is simply wrong either way. If the thick-ring design
is kept on purpose, it still needs re-exporting with **opaque** corners.
