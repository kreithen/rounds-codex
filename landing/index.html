Questions & answers

Good to know.

What Rounds Codex is, who it is for, and how it helps you learn.

What is Rounds Codex?
Rounds Codex is a visual medical education app for nursing students, medical students, and residents. It brings condition and disease guides, a 300-drug pharmacology reference, expert-curated image galleries, exam practice, and a peri-operative guide together into one clinical learning system.
Who is it for?
Three learners, one app. Rounds Codex shifts its emphasis for nursing students, medical students, and residents—so each sees the right depth at the right moment without switching tools.
What exams does it help me prepare for?
NGN-style NCLEX-RN practice for nursing students and blueprint-weighted USMLE-style practice for medical students, each with explanations written to teach the clinical reasoning—not just score the answer.
What makes it different from other study apps?
It is visual-first. Every condition pairs concise clinical text with expert-curated image galleries, and the practice questions connect back to those same libraries—so learning, understanding, and recall all live in one place.
Does Rounds Codex help with USMLE Step 1 and Step 2 CK?
Yes. Medical students get original, blueprint-weighted USMLE-style practice for Step 1 and Step 2 CK, with rationales that teach the clinical reasoning behind each answer—paired with visual condition guides so you build the mental model rather than memorizing isolated facts.
Does it include NCLEX-RN practice with Next-Gen (NGN) questions?
Yes. Nursing students get NGN-style NCLEX-RN practice focused on clinical judgment and bedside priorities, with explanations written to teach the reasoning—connected to the same visual disease guides used throughout the app.
Is there a medical image atlas?
Yes. Every condition includes an expert-curated gallery of roughly ten images spanning anatomy, pathophysiology, imaging, treatment, and clinical pearls—so you can learn medicine visually and connect what you read to what you will see on the wards.
What devices does Rounds Codex work on?
Rounds Codex is being built for the web and iOS, designed mobile-first so it works at the desk, on the wards, and at the point of care.
How much does Rounds Codex cost?
Rounds Codex is free at launch. The first 1,000 people to join the launch list lock in free access for life—no subscription, ever. After that window closes, it becomes a paid app. Join the list now to claim one of the founding free-for-life spots.
Who creates the content in Rounds Codex?
Rounds Codex is created by a practicing physician, with clinical content and imagery reviewed for medical accuracy. It is an educational resource and does not replace clinical judgment or your institution's protocols.
When does Rounds Codex launch?
It is in active development. Join the launch list below to be the first to know the moment it is available.
Does it replace clinical judgment?
No. Rounds Codex is an educational resource. It does not replace clinical judgment, supervision, or your institution's protocols.
