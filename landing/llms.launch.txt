# Rounds Codex

> Rounds Codex is a visual medical education app for nursing students, medical students, and residents. It combines clinical condition guides, a 300-drug pharmacology reference, an expert-curated medical image atlas, USMLE and NCLEX-RN practice, audio lessons, and medical calculators into one clinical learning system. Created by a practicing physician. Free to download on the Apple App Store: https://roundscodex.com/

## What it is
- A single, visual-first medical education app spanning the classroom, exams, the wards, and the point of care.
- Content is created by a practicing physician and reviewed for medical accuracy.
- It is an educational study aid and does not replace clinical judgment, supervision, or institutional protocols.

## Who it is for
- **Nursing students** — clinical judgment, bedside priorities, focused disease guides, and NGN-style NCLEX-RN practice.
- **Medical students** — high-yield visual medicine and original USMLE-style practice for Step 1 and Step 2 CK.
- **Residents** — specialty-level references and image-rich reviews across 25 specialties.

## Key features
- 1,000+ original medical illustrations — full-page artwork built to make high-yield material stick.
- 183 condition guides with Nursing, Medical, and Resident views across 25 medical and surgical specialties.
- 1,820 board-style practice questions (1,010 USMLE-style and 150 NCLEX-style) with teaching rationales.
- A 300-drug pharmacology reference (uses, dosing, half-life, adverse effects, boxed warnings, interactions).
- An expert-curated medical image atlas: roughly ten images per condition (anatomy, pathophysiology, imaging, treatment, clinical pearls).
- Audio mode — medical lessons to listen and learn hands-free.
- Medical calculators for the clinical formulas used at the bedside.
- Ongoing clinical/guideline updates so knowledge stays current.
- Works completely offline. No account required. No ads. No tracking.

## Exam preparation
- **USMLE** — blueprint-weighted, original practice questions for Step 1 and Step 2 CK with explanations that teach clinical reasoning.
- **NCLEX-RN** — Next-Generation (NGN) style items focused on clinical judgment.
